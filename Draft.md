# Draft Magic

These proposed spells and potions supplement the existing magic lists. They use the normal rules for learning and casting spells and for making alchemical potions, but remain draft material pending playtesting.

## Draft Spells

This is a second d66 spell table. Roll one die for the tens digit and another for the ones digit.

| d66 | Spell |
|---:|---|
| 11 | **Aegis Against Arrows** (range: touch): For 1 hour, ranged weapon attacks against 2 creatures per Lore skill level (1 at Lore 0) suffer Disadvantage. Siege weapons and attacks made from within 3m are unaffected. |
| 12 | **Arcane Lock** (range: touch): Magically seal one door, lid, gate, or similar closure until the next dawn. The sorcerer may name a password and any creatures who can open it normally; anyone else must dispel the spell or succeed on Physical 14+ to force it open. |
| 13 | **Binding Circle** (range: 20m): Trace a circle up to 5m across and choose demons, elementals, spirits, or undead. For 1 hour, creatures of the chosen kind cannot cross the circle in either direction unless they succeed on Lore 12+ each time they try; damaging or deliberately moving the circle ends the spell. |
| 14 | **Black Lantern** (range: 60m): A point within range radiates impenetrable magical darkness in a 10m radius for 10 minutes per Lore skill level (5 minutes at Lore 0). Ordinary light and non-magical darkvision cannot pierce it, but True Seeing can. |
| 15 | **Bloodhound Mark** (range: touch): For 24 hours, the sorcerer knows the direction and approximate distance to one touched creature while it remains on the same plane. An unwilling target negates the spell with Lore 8+, and Dispel Magic removes the mark. |
| 16 | **Breath of the Deep** (range: touch): For 2 hours per Lore skill level (1 hour at Lore 0), 2 creatures per Lore skill level (1 at Lore 0) can breathe water as easily as air and suffer no harm from ordinary deep-water pressure. This spell does not grant swimming ability or protection from cold. |
| 21 | **Bridge of Air** (range: 60m): Create an invisible, immobile walkway up to 30m long and 3m wide for 10 minutes. It may be level or gently sloped, supports any number of human-sized creatures, and vanishes immediately if dispelled. |
| 22 | **Command the Dead** (range: 30m): Up to 2 undead creatures per Lore skill level (1 at Lore 0) must throw Lore 8+ or obey the sorcerer’s spoken orders for 1 hour. An intelligent undead repeats the throw whenever ordered to harm itself or something it was sworn to protect. |
| 23 | **Cone of Winter** (range: 15m cone): Every creature in the cone suffers 4d6 cold damage, halved by a successful Physical 8+ throw. Water freezes, small mundane flames go out, and affected ground becomes difficult terrain until the ice melts. |
| 24 | **Dream Message** (range: unlimited): Send a message of no more than 25 words to a known sleeping creature on the same plane. The recipient recognizes the sorcerer, remembers the message upon waking, and may give an immediate reply of no more than 25 words. |
| 25 | **Earthshape** (range: 20m): Reshape up to a 3m cube of natural earth, clay, sand, or unworked stone into any stable form. The spell cannot shape worked masonry, precious stone, metal, or a material occupied by a living creature. |
| 26 | **Echoes of Violence** (range: touch): Touch a place or object and witness a silent, dreamlike impression of the most recent violent event involving it within the last year. The vision lasts no more than 10 minutes and reveals what occurred from the object’s location, but not hidden thoughts or events outside that viewpoint. |
| 31 | **Far Step** (range: 120m): The sorcerer and 1 willing creature per Lore skill level (the sorcerer alone at Lore 0) teleport to an unoccupied place within range that the sorcerer can see. Each traveler may carry only their normal equipment and Encumbrance. |
| 32 | **Featherfall** (range: 60m): For 10 minutes, up to 3 creatures per Lore skill level (2 at Lore 0) descend gently at 10m per round whenever they fall. A target can still jump and move normally, and the effect ends for it upon landing. |
| 33 | **Geas** (range: touch): Give a touched creature one clearly stated command that can be completed within a month; an unwilling target negates it with Lore 12+. Until the command is completed, a disobedient target suffers 2d6 damage that ignores Armor at each dawn, but a command that requires certain death or is impossible automatically fails. |
| 34 | **Ghost Door** (range: 10m): Open a passage up to 2m wide and 3m high through no more than 3m of non-magical wood, earth, or stone. The passage lasts for 10 minutes and expels occupants harmlessly to the nearer side when it closes. |
| 35 | **Gravity Well** (range: 60m): Choose a 10m-radius area. For 3 rounds, a creature that enters or begins its turn there must throw Physical 8+; on failure it is knocked prone and cannot leave the area that round, while on success all its movement within the area costs twice as much. |
| 36 | **Hearth Ward** (range: touch): Ward a campsite, room, or building no more than 20m across until the next dawn. The sorcerer wakes immediately if a creature enters without speaking a chosen password, and those resting within gain Advantage on throws to resist environmental cold and ordinary disease. |
| 41 | **Iron Tongue** (range: touch): For 1 hour, the target can speak, understand, read, and write any non-secret mortal language encountered. Codes, ciphers, magical writing, and meaning deliberately hidden through wordplay remain obscure. |
| 42 | **Mark of Return** (range: touch): Mark the ground beneath the sorcerer for 24 hours. Once before the spell ends, the sorcerer may use an action to teleport back to the mark with carried equipment and 1 willing creature per Lore skill level (no companions at Lore 0), provided all are on the same plane. |
| 43 | **Mirror Host** (range: personal): Three illusory doubles surround and mimic the sorcerer for 10 minutes. When an attack would hit the sorcerer, roll 1d6: a double is hit and destroyed on 1–3 while three remain, 1–2 while two remain, or 1 while one remains. Area effects ignore the doubles. |
| 44 | **Mute Sorcery** (range: 60m): One creature must throw Lore 8+ or lose the ability to cast spells for 1d6 rounds. This affects only spellcasting; it does not prevent the use of magical artifacts or other supernatural abilities. |
| 45 | **Night Eyes** (range: touch): For 8 hours, 2 creatures per Lore skill level (1 at Lore 0) see in dim light as if it were daylight and in darkness as if it were dim light. Magical darkness remains opaque. |
| 46 | **Oathfire** (range: touch): Two or more willing creatures swear a clearly worded bargain while touching. For one year, a creature that knowingly breaks the bargain is visibly branded and suffers modifier −2 to all throws until it makes restitution or receives Remove Curse; the spell fails if the parties do not share the same understanding of the bargain. |
| 51 | **Phantom Steed** (range: 10m): Conjure a spectral riding beast that serves one willing rider until the next dawn or dusk, whichever is later. It moves 30m per round and can cover 60km in a day, but cannot attack and vanishes after suffering any damage, dropping its rider safely in its space. |
| 52 | **Pocket Sanctuary** (range: touch): A door or similar entrance opens for 8 hours into a dry, temperate room large enough for the sorcerer and 2 companions per Lore skill level (1 companion at Lore 0). Nothing can enter except through that entrance, but occupants are expelled harmlessly when the spell ends or is dispelled. |
| 53 | **Razor Wind** (range: 60m line): A 3m-wide line of cutting air deals 3d6 damage to every creature in its path, with Physical 8+ halving the damage. Razor Wind ignores 1 point of Armor and cuts ordinary ropes, cloth, foliage, and similarly fragile obstacles. |
| 54 | **Seal the Wound** (range: touch): One living creature regains 2d6 Stamina and immediately stops bleeding. This spell cannot raise Stamina above its normal maximum, restore a lost body part, cure poison or disease, or affect the same creature more than once per day. |
| 55 | **Shadow Double** (range: 120m): Create an illusory duplicate of the sorcerer that appears at any visible point in range and lasts for 10 minutes while the sorcerer concentrates. The sorcerer sees and hears from its position and may make it move and speak, but it cannot touch anything and vanishes when struck. |
| 56 | **Silence** (range: 60m): No sound can be created in or pass through a 10m-radius sphere for 10 minutes. Creatures inside are deafened and cannot communicate by voice; spellcasting is unaffected unless a particular effect explicitly requires speech or sound. |
| 61 | **Sleep of Ages** (range: 60m): Choose up to 3 creatures per Lore skill level (2 at Lore 0); each must throw Lore 8+ or fall into enchanted sleep for 1 hour. A sleeping target wakes if injured or if another creature spends an action vigorously rousing it, and creatures that do not sleep are immune. |
| 62 | **Stone Rampart** (range: 40m): Raise a rough stone wall up to 20m long, 3m high, and 1m thick from natural ground. Each 3m section has Defense 6, Armor 4, and 20 Stamina; the wall is permanent but cannot appear in an occupied space or topple directly onto a creature. |
| 63 | **Storm Shelter** (range: 10m): For 8 hours, an immobile 10m-radius dome turns aside ordinary rain, wind, snow, airborne ash, and dangerous natural temperatures. The dome does not stop creatures, attacks, magical weather, floodwater, or a lack of breathable air. |
| 64 | **Unseen Hand** (range: 30m): For 10 minutes while concentrating, move one unattended object weighing no more than 100kg at up to 10m per round. Fine manipulation is possible with a Craft 8+ throw, but the hand cannot attack, activate a magical item, or wrest an object from a creature. |
| 65 | **Water Walk** (range: touch): For 1 hour, 2 creatures per Lore skill level (1 at Lore 0) and their carried equipment can stand and move across liquid as if it were firm ground. A target may choose to end the effect on itself at any time. |
| 66 | **Web of Night** (range: 60m): Fill a 10m-radius area with sticky shadow-webs for 10 minutes. A creature entering or starting its turn there must throw Physical 8+ or become restrained; a restrained creature or adjacent ally may use an action and a successful Physical 8+ throw to free it. Fire clears a 3m section but deals 1d6 damage to every creature in that section. |

## Draft Alchemical Potions

These entries can form a replacement 2d6 discovery table or be added individually to treasure and formula lists. Their Tier is the Wealth Tier normally required to purchase a dose when one is available.

| 2d6 | Tier | Alchemical Potion |
|---:|---:|---|
| 2 | 5 | **Phoenix Cordial:** For the next 8 hours, the imbiber is surrounded by a faint odor of smoke. The first time they would fall to 0 Stamina during that period, they instead remain at 1 Stamina and the effect ends; instant death still kills them. |
| 3 | 4 | **Beast-Tongue Broth:** For 1 hour, the imbiber can converse with ordinary animals. Animals are not compelled to be friendly, intelligent, truthful, or knowledgeable beyond their experience. |
| 4 | 3 | **Cat’s-Eye Elixir:** For 8 hours, the imbiber sees in dim light as if it were daylight and in darkness as if it were dim light. Bright daylight causes Disadvantage on sight-based throws during this time unless the imbiber shades their eyes. |
| 5 | 3 | **Gecko Tonic:** For 1 hour, the imbiber can climb walls and ceilings at half normal movement without a throw, provided at least two hands and feet are bare. Slippery, crumbling, or magically smooth surfaces still require Physical 8+. |
| 6 | 3 | **Deep-Lung Cordial:** For 2 hours, the imbiber can breathe water as easily as air and gains Advantage on Physical throws to resist inhaled poison, smoke, and suffocation. The potion grants no swimming ability or protection from pressure and cold. |
| 7 | 4 | **Giant’s Ichor:** For 1 hour, the imbiber gains modifier +2 on Physical throws based on raw strength, modifier +2 to melee damage, and triple normal carrying capacity. When the effect ends, the imbiber becomes Fatigued until completing 8 hours of rest. |
| 8 | 4 | **Ironhide Decoction:** The imbiber’s skin becomes gray and hard, granting Armor 2 for 1 hour; this does not stack with worn armor or Sorcerer’s Armor. During that hour the imbiber cannot swim and suffers Disadvantage on Stealth throws that depend on moving silently. |
| 9 | 4 | **Oracle’s Tear:** After drinking, the imbiber may ask the Referee one question about the likely consequence of a specific action they intend to attempt within the next hour. The Referee gives a brief, truthful omen based on the most likely outcome; hidden choices and genuinely random events may make the omen incomplete. |
| 10 | 4 | **Thunderbelly Philter:** Within 1 hour of drinking, the imbiber may use one action to exhale lightning in a 15m line. Every creature in the line suffers 4d6 damage, halved by Physical 8+; if the charge is not used before the hour ends, the imbiber suffers 1d6 damage that ignores Armor. |
| 11 | 5 | **Truthglass Draught:** For 10 minutes, the imbiber understands any spoken language and knows when a creature they can hear knowingly speaks a direct falsehood. The potion does not reveal omissions, mistakes, evasions, written lies, or the truth itself. |
| 12 | 5 | **Wraith Oil:** Applying this oil to a creature and its equipment takes 1 minute. For the next 10 minutes, the target may pass through non-magical wood, earth, or stone, but cannot end movement inside it; if the effect expires while the target is within solid matter, they are expelled by the shortest route and suffer 3d6 damage that ignores Armor. |
