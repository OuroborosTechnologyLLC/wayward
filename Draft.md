# Treasure Encumbrance

Treasure counts toward Encumbrance until secured in a settlement, stronghold, or other safe repository. Banked WP do not.

Each typical unbanked WP fills 5 item spaces, divided into five 1-space bundles. Apply Encumbrance normally. Five secured bundles add 1 WP to Wealth.

> **Referee Guidance:** Five spaces represent sacks of mixed treasure, not a fixed weight. Compact treasure may fill 1 space per WP; bulky treasure may fill 10 or more. State unusual bulk when found. Lost, abandoned, or destroyed bundles grant no WP.

## Equipment

| Item | Tier | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Small backpack | 1 | Carries 2 items without Encumbrance. |
| Backpack | 2 | Carries 4 items without Encumbrance. |
| Large backpack | 3 | Carries 6 items without Encumbrance. |
| Small sack | 1 | Holds 1 bundle (1/5 WP). Counts as 1 item when filled. |
| Large sack | 1 | Holds 5 bundles (1 WP). Counts as 5 items and requires two hands when filled. |
