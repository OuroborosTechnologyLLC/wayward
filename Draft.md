# UPDATE: Introduction

The world ebbs and flows between order and chaos. Civilizations rise, drive roads through the wilderness, and raise walls against everything they fear. Then the gold runs thin, the rulers grow fat, and the walls begin to crack. Dark ages and golden ages matter little to those waiting for the right moment to strike.

Beyond the frontier, hard people wrest a living from hard country. They hunt, herd, trade, raid, guide caravans, and sell their swords to kings who despise them but cannot win a war without them. Within the cities, thieves climb the roofs, smugglers ply the rivers, and sorcerers bargain with powers best left unnamed. Priests promise salvation. Nobles promise payment. Neither promise is worth much.

Sooner or later, every wanderer sees civilization for what it is: a grand feast with too few seats and far too many knives. Some turn away in disgust. Others draw up a chair. There are fortunes beneath ruined temples, secrets in forgotten tombs, and thrones whose occupants have mistaken inheritance for strength. Opportunity is everywhere—for anyone bold, clever, or desperate enough to seize it.

These are the people who survive where armies perish. Barbarians and mercenaries, cutpurses and outcasts, faithless scoundrels and dangerous heroes. They may rescue a city, rob it, conquer it, or burn it down before breakfast. Most will do whichever pays best.

*Wayward!* is a sword-and-sorcery roleplaying game about such heroes—or, more often, antiheroes. Test steel, nerve, and forbidden lore against cruel warlords, hungry beasts, decadent empires, and ancient things that should have stayed buried. Win glory if you can. Take the gold either way.

So gather your dice, sharpen your blade, and keep one eye on your companions. The road is calling. Go... **Wayward!**
